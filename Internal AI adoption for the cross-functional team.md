# Internal AI Adoption for a Cross-Functional Team

From individual practices to one shared way of working with Claude.
--


## Overview
A small cross-functional team adopted Claude Cowork without a shared setup or documented practices. Technical skill varied widely, prompting was inconsistent, and the knowledge base was not kept up to date.

**Focus:** Role-based onboarding, clear task ownership, and a pre-delivery quality check for client-facing work.

**Approach:** Team interviews → use-case and workflow mapping → role-based documentation. I mapped use cases by role and project stage, then built the documentation around roles rather than tool features.


## Documentation Deliverables

| Deliverable | What it gave the team |
|---|---|
| **Setup guide** | A role-specific path: what to set up and what to do next |
| **Responsibility map** | Clear task ownership, including who maintains the shared Claude knowledge base |
| **Use-case walkthrough** | A real project task shown step by step |
| **Troubleshooting guide** | A clear route when something breaks |
| **Pre-delivery checklist** | What to verify before AI-assisted work goes to a client |



## Outcome
The team moved from individual practices to a documented, role-based workflow with defined responsibilities, consistent guidance, and a shared approach to reviewing client-facing work.
